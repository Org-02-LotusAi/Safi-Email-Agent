# How to Use Your Safi Assistants — User Guide

Welcome! You now have your own AI assistants that you work with **entirely by email**. There's
nothing to install and no app to log into — you just send them emails like you would a colleague,
and they reply. This short guide shows you how.

---

## What you get

You have two personal assistants:

- **Reply Assistant** — helps you handle correspondence: draft replies, summarise threads, answer
  questions, and respond to emails you forward it.
- **Schedule Assistant** — helps with meetings and scheduling: proposing times, confirming
  details, and drafting scheduling replies.

Each assistant is **private to you**. What you tell it and what it learns is yours alone — other
people have their own separate assistants.

> When you first sign up, you'll receive a welcome email with your assistants' email addresses.
> Keep that email handy — those addresses are how you reach them.

---

## The two things you can do

### 1. Ask it to do work
Just email an assistant with your request, like you'd email a person. Use any subject line that
describes what you need.

**Examples of what you can send:**
- "Can you draft a polite reply saying we'll get back to them next week?"
- Forward an email thread and write: "Please draft a good reply to this."
- "Suggest three times I could meet next week for a 30-minute call."
- Attach a document and ask: "Summarise the key points in the attached file."

You'll get a reply back by email, usually within a minute.

### 2. Teach it how you like things (optional)
You can train your assistant to match your style and rules. To do this, send it an email with the
subject line set to exactly:

> **Private**

Then write your instructions in plain English. For example:

- "Always keep replies short and professional."
- "Sign off my emails as 'Warm regards, Jane'."
- "When scheduling, only propose times Monday–Friday, 9am–5pm, and always confirm my timezone."
- "Never discuss pricing unless the customer asks first."

You'll get a short confirmation, and from then on your assistant follows your guidance on every
future reply. You can send as many of these as you like — it keeps learning. Only **your**
assistant changes; no one else's is affected.

---

## Working with documents and forwarded emails

- **Attachments:** You can attach **Word (.docx), PDF, or text (.txt)** files to any request, and
  your assistant will read them and use them in its reply.
- **Forwarded emails:** Forward any email conversation and ask for help — your assistant reads the
  history and uses it to craft a relevant response.
- **Reference material:** If you attach a document in a **Private** email (e.g. your standard
  terms or a product sheet), your assistant remembers it and can use it later without you having
  to attach it again.

---

## What to expect

- **It remembers your conversations.** Your assistant keeps track of your ongoing topics, so
  follow-up emails on the same subject build on what came before.
- **It stays on topic and private.** It only uses what's relevant to you and your current
  conversation, and it never shares your private instructions with the people you're writing to.
- **It replies by email only.** Your assistant drafts and sends email replies — it won't take
  other actions for you (it won't book meetings in your calendar, make purchases, or change any
  of your accounts). You stay in control of what actually gets done.

---

## Tips for the best results

- **Be clear about what you want.** "Draft a two-sentence reply declining politely" beats "reply
  to this."
- **Give context when it helps.** Forward the relevant thread or attach the document.
- **Set your preferences once.** A few `Private` instructions up front (tone, sign-off, rules)
  make every future reply feel like you.
- **Review before you send on.** Your assistant drafts the reply; you decide whether to use it
  as-is or tweak it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. Everything happens over email.

**Is my information private?**
Yes. Your assistant and everything it learns are personal to you and isolated from other users.

**Can other people change my assistant?**
No. Only emails from you can train your assistant.

**What if I want to start fresh?**
Just ask your administrator to reset your assistant, or send new `Private` instructions that
override the old ones.

**How fast will I get a reply?**
Usually within a minute, sometimes a little longer for complex requests or large attachments.

**Which assistant should I email?**
Use the **Reply Assistant** for general correspondence and the **Schedule Assistant** for
anything to do with meetings and timing. If in doubt, either one will do its best to help.
